# Poster Studio — Free Social Media Poster Maker

**Turn any photo into a branded social media poster in seconds.** Upload a picture, pick a frame, and Poster Studio fits it into a ready-made design that already carries your logo and contact details. Change the colors, text and layout, then download a sharp PNG or JPG.

It runs entirely in your browser. There is nothing to install, no account to create, no watermark, and your images never leave your computer.

## Why Poster Studio?

Small businesses, shops, rental services, event organisers and freelancers post the same kind of graphic again and again: _a photo, a headline, and the contact details at the bottom._ Rebuilding that in a design tool every time is slow, and keeping your branding consistent is harder still.

Poster Studio solves that with **reusable frames**:

- Set up your **logo and contact bar once**. They are remembered and appear on every poster.
- Drop in a new photo and it is **automatically fitted** to the frame.
- Everything is **editable**, so one frame can look completely different with new colors, fonts and text.

Typical uses: _"3 Ton Pickup for Rent"_ ads, product promotions, sale announcements, real-estate listings, event posters, restaurant specials, service advertisements and daily social posts.

## Quick start

### Option 1: use it online

Open **[posterstudioapp.vercel.app](https://posterstudioapp.vercel.app/)**. Nothing to download or sign up for.

### Option 2: run it on your computer

1. Download or clone this project.
2. Double-click **`index.html`**.

That is all. It opens in your default browser and works straight away.

### Option 3: run a tiny local server (optional)

Handy if you prefer `http://localhost`:

```bash
# Python 3
python -m http.server 8000

# or Node.js
npx serve .
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8000`.

> **Internet note:** the optional Google Fonts (Poppins, Montserrat, Oswald and others) are loaded from Google, so those fonts need an internet connection. Offline, Poster Studio falls back to a system font and everything else still works.

## How to make your first poster

1. **Upload your photo.** Press **Photo** (top right), drop an image onto the preview, or paste one with `Ctrl+V`.
2. **Choose a size.** Use the size menu at the top, for example _Instagram Post, 1080×1080_.
3. **Pick a frame** in the **Layout** tab. Click any template to apply it.
4. **Add your brand** in the **Brand** tab: upload your logo and edit your phone, email, website and address.
5. **Write your headline** in the **Text** tab and try a quick style.
6. **Choose a color theme** in the **Colors** tab.
7. **Reposition the photo.** Drag it on the preview and scroll to zoom.
8. Press **Download** and choose PNG or JPG.

The first four steps take about a minute. After that, your logo and contact details are remembered, so a new poster only needs a new photo and headline.

## Editing text and effects

Open the **Text** tab to edit the **Headline** and **Subtitle** separately.

![Text tab with headline styling, quick styles, fonts and effects](docs/screenshots/04-text-styling.png)

**Quick styles (one click):** Clean · Soft shadow · Hard shadow · Neon glow · Outline · Highlight · Gradient

**Fine control:**

- **Type:** font (21 choices), weight (Regular to Black), color, size, letter spacing, line height, UPPERCASE and italic.
- **Gradient fill:** blend two colors across the text.
- **Shadow or glow:** color, opacity, blur, X and Y offset. Zero offset with blur makes a neon glow.
- **Outline:** color and thickness.
- **Highlight box:** a colored label behind each line, with padding and rounded corners.
- **Placement:** top, middle or bottom, with left, center or right alignment, plus an optional dark gradient behind the text so white text stays readable on bright photos.

![Text tab effects: gradient fill, shadow or glow, outline, highlight box and placement](docs/screenshots/05-text-effects.png)

**Fonts included:** Poppins, Montserrat, Roboto, Raleway, Oswald, Bebas Neue, Anton, Archivo Black, Playfair Display, Lora, Abril Fatface, Lobster, Pacifico, Dancing Script, Permanent Marker, Righteous, plus the system fonts Segoe UI, Arial, Georgia, Impact and Verdana.

## Brand: logo and contact bar

This is the heart of the app: set it up **once** and it is remembered next time you open Poster Studio.

![Brand tab with logo upload, placement options and an editable contact list](docs/screenshots/06-brand-contact-bar.png)

**Logo**

- Upload a PNG, JPG or SVG. A transparent PNG looks best.
- Place it in the bar (left or top-centered) or on the photo (top-left or top-right).
- Resize it with the _Logo height_ slider.

**Contact bar**

- The bar starts with two items, a **phone number** and a **website**. Press **+ Add contact item** to add more.
- Six icon types: **Phone, Email, Website, Address, Handle (@), Instagram**.
- Switch any item on or off, reorder it with the arrows, delete it, or add as many as you need.
- Choose 1 to 4 columns (or Auto), text size, font and icon style (circle, plain or none).
- Long text shrinks to fit automatically, and the bar grows or shrinks to match its contents.

## Photo controls

![Photo tab with upload, zoom and adjustment sliders](docs/screenshots/03-photo-controls.png)

- **Drag** the photo on the preview to choose what is visible.

- Works with JPG, PNG, WebP and other image formats your browser can open.

## Saving, sharing and exporting

![Saved tab with named designs, load, delete, export and import](docs/screenshots/08-saved-designs.png)

**Downloading your poster**

![Download menu with PNG and JPG options and a 1x or 2x size switch](docs/screenshots/09-download-menu.png)

- Choose **PNG** (best quality) or **JPG** (smaller file).
- Choose **1× size** or **2× sharp** for crisp results on high-resolution screens and in print.
- **The file name comes from your headline.** _"3 Ton Pickup for Rent!"_ becomes `3-ton-pickup-for-rent.png`. Spaces and punctuation turn into single dashes, and accents are removed. If the headline is off or empty, the subtitle is used, and if both are empty the file is called `poster.png`.
- Very large 2× exports (above about 40 megapixels) automatically fall back to 1× so the browser does not run out of memory.

**Saving designs**

- Your latest settings (layout, colors, text, logo and contacts) are **remembered automatically**.
- Use the **Saved** tab to store named designs such as "Weekend sale" or "New listing", then load or delete them later.
- **Export .json** creates a small template file you can back up or send to a teammate. **Import .json** loads it again.
- Saved designs include everything _except the photo_, so a template stays small and reusable.

## Privacy and your data

- **Everything happens locally in your browser.** Photos and logos are never uploaded to any server.
- Settings, your logo and saved designs are stored in your browser's **localStorage** on your own device. Clearing site data removes them, so use **Export .json** for backups.
- The only network request the app makes is loading **Google Fonts** for the optional web fonts. If you prefer no external requests, delete the Google Fonts `<link>` tags in `index.html`; the app will use system fonts.
- There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or trackers.

## Browser support

Poster Studio uses the HTML Canvas API and works in current versions of **Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari** on desktop and mobile.

It relies on modern canvas features (`roundRect`, `letterSpacing` and canvas `filter`). If one is missing in an older browser, the app degrades gracefully, for example by skipping photo adjustments or letter spacing. For the best results, keep your browser up to date.

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

**My fonts look different from the screenshots.**
The web fonts load from Google Fonts. Check your internet connection and refresh. The font menu previews each font once loaded.

**Can I use it offline?**
Yes. Everything works offline except the Google web fonts, which fall back to system fonts.

**Where did my saved designs go?**
They live in your browser's local storage. Clearing browser data, using a private window or switching browsers or computers means they will not be there. Use **Export .json** to keep a backup.

**My logo has a white box around it.**
Use a PNG with a transparent background.

**The download did not start.**
Allow downloads for the page if your browser asks. Very large canvases (for example A4 at 2×) can hit browser memory limits, so try 1×.

**Why is the photo not included in saved designs?**
Photos can be several megabytes and browsers limit local storage. Designs are meant to be reusable frames, so you add a fresh photo each time.

**Does it add a watermark?**
No. Your poster is exported exactly as you see it.

**Is it free for commercial use?**
Yes, under the MIT License. Check the license of any fonts and images you use in your posters.
